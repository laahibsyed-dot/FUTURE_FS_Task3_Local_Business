# Short Pitch — Spice Route Kitchen

## Business problem
A local restaurant can lose potential customers when people cannot quickly see the menu, opening hours, location or an easy way to reserve a table online.

## Website solution
This website gives the restaurant a professional online presence with:
- A strong first impression and clear **Reserve a Table** button
- Popular dishes visible without searching
- Weekend offer section to promote higher-value family visits
- Mobile-friendly layout for customers browsing on phones
- Reservation form that captures customer interest
- Contact, opening hours and location information in one place

## How it can help the business grow
1. **More enquiries:** Clear call-to-action buttons make it easier for visitors to contact the restaurant.
2. **Better discoverability:** A dedicated website can be shared from Google Business Profile, Instagram, WhatsApp and other channels.
3. **Higher engagement:** Menu and special-offer sections encourage customers to explore before visiting.
4. **Better customer experience:** Important information is available in a few seconds on mobile and desktop.
5. **Stronger brand image:** Consistent typography, spacing and colours create a professional presentation.

## Owner pitch
“Your restaurant already has the food and local customers. This website makes it easier for new customers to discover you, see your best dishes, check your timings and request a table from their phone. It also gives you a professional link to share on Google, Instagram and WhatsApp. The goal is simple: turn online visitors into real enquiries and visits.”
